How we build campaigns together
Ownership, the request path, and what every campaign needs.
Every campaign sits in one structure
And maps to one approved theme, named [REGION]-[ICP]-[THEME].
Business objective
The big-rock priority it ladders up to.
Campaign (theme)
The ongoing initiative. [REGION]-[ICP]-[THEME].
Channel or source
Where we reach them: paid social, paid search, email, events.
HubSpot feature
Forms, ads, scoring and workflows that capture and report it.
Why it matters. The theme name is the thread that ties spend, leads and pipeline together. If the theme is missing or wrong, the campaign will not show up in reporting.
Who owns what
Performance owns search
Evergreen, always-on search sits with performance all year. Beyond that it is collaborative: you bring the keywords, we build and optimise. Requests fold into the shared evergreen campaigns so the data compounds.
Growth teams commission paid social
LinkedIn campaigns plus event and content bursts. You bring the brief and assets, performance builds it and wires up the tracking.
You own the HubSpot campaign. Create it for your initiative, named to the theme. Performance links the paid UTMs and leads, so it stays yours.
One request path for everything: the PPC Campaign Request form.
Three things every campaign needs before it goes live
A working conversion
The action it drives is set up and firing. Verified before launch, not after.
A tracked form
A live landing page with a HubSpot form that captures the UTM hidden fields. No 404s, no redirects.
A clean UTM link
Built in the HubSpot URL Builder to the naming standard, not hand-typed or numeric.
When these three are set, the campaign launches clean and reports properly. If one is missing, we sort it together before going live.
How to request a campaign
Submit via the PPC Campaign Request form.
Build needs 3 to 5 working days before your deadline.
What to provide
•Market, product and ICP
•The theme it maps to, from the approved list
•Goal and the conversion it should drive
•The live landing page URL, with a HubSpot form on it
•Lead routing: nurture, SDR, or both
•Assets and copy
•Deadline
One structure. Consistent setup. Better reporting.
Set it up right at the request and it shows up everywhere it should: the Monday snapshot, pipeline, and the campaigns that earn more budget.
